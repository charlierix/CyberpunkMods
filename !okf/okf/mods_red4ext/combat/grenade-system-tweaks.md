# Grenade System Tweaks

Modifying ImmersiveGrenades.* TweakDB records to alter grenade mechanics.

## Approach

Mods modify `ImmersiveGrenades.*` TweakDB records to change grenade fuse times, blast radii, damage values, or throwing mechanics.

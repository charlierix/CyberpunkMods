# Healing System Tweaks

Modifying ImmersiveHealing.* TweakDB records to alter healing item behavior.

## Approach

Mods modify `ImmersiveHealing.*` TweakDB records to change healing item mechanics. This includes modified heal amounts, healing over time, or custom healing item behavior.

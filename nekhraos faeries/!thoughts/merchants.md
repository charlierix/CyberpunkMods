# rough ideas

## queens n pillars
imagine pillars placed somewhat randomly, so many layers that you can't see past all of them.  sort of a queens n arrangement so that it becomes a wall of pillars

now turn those pillars into elongated capsules.  instead of a 2D sheet of pillars, it's a fuzzy spherical shell around the player

don't just make the room into a big hollow sphere.  make multiple rooms, but they are just suggestions of rooms - low density of pillars compared to the walls that are higher density

this will also be zero g and somewhat viscous, slightly foggy.  the player will have full 3D rotation and movement

use color and light to help make the different directions make sense

#### specialties
- gravity manipulation
- time dilation
